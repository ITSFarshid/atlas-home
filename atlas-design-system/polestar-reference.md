# Polestar 2 reference decisions

Source: https://www.polestar.com/uk/polestar-2/technology/#technology

Inspected official centre-display home and Drive settings images:

- https://www.polestar.com/dato-assets/94392/1742202701-07-1-polestar-2-26-technology-displays-centre-d.png
- https://www.polestar.com/dato-assets/94392/1741856020-08-1-polestar-2-26-technology-android-automotive-drive-d.png

Observed: a black base, rectilinear charcoal tiles, sparse separators, regular white sans-serif text, imagery within home tiles and orange selection in settings. These observations describe the referenced screens, not every Polestar model or software version.

Adapted: flat rectangular surfaces, restrained white/grey typography, orange actions and selected indicators. Colours and sizes were chosen for our prototype rather than extracted as authoritative manufacturer values.

Retained from our project: landscape zoning, fixed map and widget dimensions, native clock/navigation rail, equal feature availability, existing driving restrictions and blue map route. We did not adopt the portrait four-tile arrangement, add Google services, use Polestar branding or change climate functionality.

Evaluation: the result is closer to the reference in shape, surface treatment, type weight and control states. Its information architecture intentionally remains the current mixed-grid candidate. The exact font, display materials and production interaction behavior have not been replicated.
